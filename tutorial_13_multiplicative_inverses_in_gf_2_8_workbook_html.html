Inverse definition \(a \times a^{-1} = \texttt{0x01}\) in \(\mathrm{GF}(2^8)\)
Find \(b\) such that \(a \times b = \texttt{0x01}\).
The multiplicative identity \(\texttt{0x01}\) is the identity: \(a \times \texttt{0x01} = a\) for all \(a\).
Every nonzero element has a unique inverse.
Special case: 0x00 \(\texttt{0x00}\) has no inverse.
It is the additive identity, not invertible under multiplication.
Symmetry of inverse pairs If \(a^{-1} = b\), then \(b^{-1} = a\).
\(a \times b = \texttt{0x01} \Leftrightarrow b \times a = \texttt{0x01}\).
