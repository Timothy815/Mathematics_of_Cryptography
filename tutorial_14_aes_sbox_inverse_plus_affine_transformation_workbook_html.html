S-box construction Stage 1: find the \(\mathrm{GF}(2^8)\) inverse.
Stage 2: apply the affine transformation.
\(\mathrm{S}(a) = \mathrm{Affine}(a^{-1})\)
Special case: S-box(0x00) = 0x63 \(\texttt{0x00}\) has no inverse, so Stage 1 yields \(\texttt{0x00}\).
XORing all-zero bits with constant \(\texttt{0x63}\) gives \(\texttt{0x63}\).
Affine formula \(s_i = b_i \oplus b_{(i+4)\%8} \oplus b_{(i+5)\%8} \oplus b_{(i+6)\%8} \oplus b_{(i+7)\%8} \oplus c_i\)
where \(b\) is the inverse byte and \(c_i\) are bits of \(\texttt{0x63}\).
Affine constant: 0x63 \(\texttt{0x63} = 01100011_2\).
It is XORed in at the end of the affine step (bit by bit via \(c_i\)).
